# Sample part models

The STEP export dialog's preview board places these two models:

- `R_0603.step`: the 0603 resistor model from the project's own HoverBall_power board (its embedded
  "0603 resistor.STEP"), unchanged.
- `PinHeader_1x02_P2.54mm_Vertical.step`: from the KiCad 3D model library
  (https://gitlab.com/kicad/libraries/kicad-packages3D, Connector_PinHeader_2.54mm.3dshapes),
  unchanged. Licensed under Creative Commons CC-BY-SA 4.0, with the KiCad library's exception for
  electronic designs that use it; the full terms are in the file's header.
